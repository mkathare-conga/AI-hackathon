## Slide 2: The Problem

### Slide title

Revenue leaks after signature when contract truth is not operationalized

### On-slide bullets

- Billing often does not reflect the latest commercial term
- Amendments change pricing, but downstream systems keep old assumptions
- Renewal notice windows are missed even when uplifts were contractually allowed
- Teams lack one trusted commercial source of truth across documents and systems

### Speaker cue

Use one example only: a contract allows a 5% uplift, billing stays flat, and the company misses revenue it already earned the right to charge.
