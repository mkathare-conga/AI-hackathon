## Slide 5: Why AI Is Actually Needed

### Slide title

AI reads the contract, deterministic logic counts the money

### On-slide bullets

- AI extracts renewal, uplift, notice, and override language
- AI resolves meaning across versions and amendments
- Deterministic logic handles deadlines, math, and impact
- Hybrid approach is explainable and reproducible

### Short contrast

- Pure rules are brittle on messy documents
- Pure LLM reasoning is too loose for financial calculations

## Slide 6: How We Resolve Conflicting Documents

### Slide title

Conflicting documents are resolved, not just retrieved

### On-slide bullets

- AI gets the first pass across the full dossier
- If AI does not return a winner, deterministic fallback takes over
- Explicit override language wins first
- Amendment beats renewal notice beats order form beats MSA
- Higher version and stronger evidence break ties

### Demo-backed examples

- Summit amendment v4 overrides earlier 4%, 6%, and 8% pricing references
